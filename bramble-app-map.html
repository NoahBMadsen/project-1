The App

A foraging companion where you identify plants, log your finds, quiz yourself on what you've learned, and discover community-pinned spots near you — all free, no paywall.

Target User
Beginner and intermediate foragers — teens, young adults, and outdoors lovers — who want a personal field guide and a way to connect with their local foraging community.
Core Features
  • 1
    Plant Browser
    Search plants with ID tips, edibility notes, and safety warnings.
  • 2
    Personal Journal
    Log finds with date, location, and notes. Grows into your own field guide.
  • 3
    Community Map
    Drop pins on foraging spots. Browse nearby finds with seasonal filters.
  • 4
    Plant Quiz
    Identify plants from descriptions or images pulled from your own log. Active learning.
  • 5
    My Field Guide
    A personal, growing collection of every plant you've encountered.

What Makes It Different
Bramble's edge: The only free app purpose-built for foragers — combining a personal log, community map, quiz, and seasonal awareness in one place. No paywall, no algorithm, no noise.

Name origin: a bramble is a wild thorny plant (blackberries, raspberries). Rugged, outdoor, immediately plant-forward.

Competing Apps
PictureThis Paywall after free uses. No foraging map, no personal journal, not foraging-specific.

PlantNet ID-only. No community, no journal, no edibility/safety focus.
